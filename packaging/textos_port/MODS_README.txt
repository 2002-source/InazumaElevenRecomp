MODS - INAZUMA ELEVEN RECOMP  v1.1.1
====================================
(Español: LEEME.txt - Français : LISEZMOI.txt - Deutsch: LIESMICH.txt -
Italiano: LEGGIMI.txt)

Turn mods on or off in the launcher (Mods) or in the in-game Esc menu (Mods).
Changes apply when the game starts or after Esc > System > Restart.
In the launcher, press Tab or right-click a mod to see what it does.
mods.cfg lists the active mods, one per line. The preparer turns on all the
included mods except "Passion and Friendship max".


FREE TRAINING  (folder: entrenamiento_libre)
  A trainer on top of the Tower opens a menu to train your players: GP, TP and
  stats in exchange for Passion (450 per point).
  Y: level up the player paying Passion (225 per stat point gained, at least
     100 per level); the stats, GP, TP and moves are the ones the game gives.
  X: the player goes back to level 1 for free: keeps the moves learned and
     loses all training (with IE2 mechanics, the moves go back to 0 uses).
  How to get it: finish the story (win the Football Frontier) and go up to the
  top of the Tower.
  Created from: your English ROM.

IE2 MECHANICS  (folder: mecanicas_ie2)
  Inazuma Eleven 2 mechanics, using the game's own code (it was switched off):
  - Move levels: special moves level up with use ("Lv +" notice and the level
    mark next to the name, in menus and when the move is used).
  - Shooting: shoot button on the touch screen (you can drag it), long shots
    from outside the box (L/T mark next to the name) and the blue shooting
    area.
  - Shot blocks with block or shoot moves (B mark).
  - Talents (Saving, Ultra Moves...) with their effect in matches.
  - After finishing the story, Mark is no longer fixed: he can leave the team
    and the first spot.
  - In random encounters, winning by 5 or more goals without conceding doubles
    the experience ("x2!!" on every player, total in magenta).
  Your save file does not change, except each move's use count (the game
  already saved it).
  How to get it: always on.
  Created from: your English ROM (plus the IE2 data already included).

TOTAL POWER IN SPECIAL MOVES  (folder: valor_escenas)
  Shows each player's total power during special move scenes: both values in
  dribble, block and shot-block duels, the shot's value and, when it reaches
  the keeper, how much power it arrives with (including distance) against the
  save. The winner is shown in red.
  How to get it: always on, in any match.

INSTANT SCOUTING  (folder: ojeo_instantaneo)
  When Celia or Nelly search for players for you, the call arrives right away
  instead of after several minutes of play.
  How to get it: always on.
  Created from: your English ROM.

SPECIAL MOVE FILTERS  (folder: filtros_supertecnicas)
  An X "Filters" button in the special move manuals list (Inventory > Special
  moves > Learn): filter by type and element, and sort by TP or by name.
  How to get it: always on.

AFFINITY COLOURS  (folder: colores_afinidad)
  Special move names in the colour of their element, like in Victory Road:
  fire red, wind blue, wood green and mountain brown; talents in purple. On
  the move's scene banner, on the move buttons in matches and in the menus.
  How to get it: always on.

JAPANESE VOICES  (folder: voces_jp)
  The original Japanese voices: cutscenes (and the Japanese opening) and the
  players' voices in matches.
  How to get it: always on once created.
  Created from: the Japanese ROM (Inazuma Eleven, code YEEJ).

PASSION AND FRIENDSHIP MAX  (folder: pasion_amistad_max) - off at first
  Keeps Passion and Friendship at 999999. Turn it on only if you want it:
  what you earn while it is on stays in your save.


IF A MOD IS MISSING OR DOES NOTHING
  Drag the ROMs again onto "PREPARAR - arrastra aqui tus ROM.bat" (in the main
  folder). It rebuilds the mods from them:
  - Free training, IE2 mechanics or Instant scouting ... drag the English ROM
                                                          (YEEP).
  - Trainer dialogue in Spanish ......................... drag the English AND
                                                          the Spanish ROM
                                                          (YEES) together.
  - Japanese voices ..................................... drag the Japanese
                                                          ROM (YEEJ).
  Then check that the mod is turned on (launcher > Mods).
